Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Our Founding Commitment

Aequitas Market will never sell, share, or monetize your personal data. There are no advertisements on this platform. Your data is used solely to operate the marketplace — nothing more. This is a founding commitment baked into the architecture of our company, not a policy that changes with management or market pressure.

1. Who We Are

Aequitas Market is operated by Praktikal 03, LLC, headquartered in Astoria, Oregon. When this policy refers to "we," "us," or "our," it means Praktikal 03, LLC.

Contact: contact@aequitasmarket.shop

2. What We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the marketplace:

Data Why We Need It Shared With
Name, email Account creation, communication No one
Password (hashed) Authentication No one — stored encrypted
Shipping address Order fulfillment Seller (for shipping only)
Payment information Process transactions Stripe (payment processor only)
Listing content Display items for sale Public (visible on marketplace)
Order history Track purchases and sales No one

3. What We Never Do

  • Sell your data — to anyone, for any reason, ever
  • Share data with advertisers — there are no ads on this platform
  • Use tracking pixels or third-party analytics that profile your behavior
  • Build behavioral profiles for targeting or recommendations algorithms
  • Monetize your browsing activity, search history, or purchase patterns
  • Sell or share data in the event of acquisition — any acquirer must honor this commitment

4. No Advertisements

Aequitas Market does not display advertisements of any kind. There are no banner ads, sponsored listings, promoted placements, or pay-to-play visibility features. Every listing ranks on merit — not money. This is a founding architectural decision, not a temporary policy.

5. Payment Processing

Payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor. Your credit card details are never stored on our servers — they are handled entirely by Stripe's secure infrastructure. We receive only the information necessary to confirm and track transactions.

For Stripe's privacy practices, see: stripe.com/privacy

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Supabase, a secure cloud database platform with row-level security policies. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and are never stored in plain text.

We implement the principle of least privilege — our systems access only the minimum data necessary to perform their function.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correct — update inaccurate information in your profile
  • Delete — request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export — receive your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@aequitasmarket.shop. We will respond within 14 days.

8. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for the platform to function (authentication session). We do not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or third-party cookies of any kind.

9. Children's Privacy

Aequitas Market is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify registered users via email of any material changes at least 14 days before they take effect. Our founding commitment to zero data selling will never change — it is a permanent, non-negotiable principle of this company.
